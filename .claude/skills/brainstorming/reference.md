# Brainstorming Techniques Reference

## Free Association
- Generate ideas without judgment or filtering
- Build on others' ideas ("yes, and...")
- Encourage wild and unconventional thoughts
- Focus on quantity initially
- Defer evaluation until later

## SCAMPER
**S**ubstitute: What can be replaced?
**C**ombine: What can be merged?
**A**dapt: What can be adjusted from elsewhere?
**M**odify: What can be changed or magnified?
**P**ut to another use: How else can this be used?
**E**liminate: What can be removed?
**R**everse: What if we did the opposite?

## Reverse Brainstorming
1. Identify the problem
2. Ask: "How could we cause or worsen this problem?"
3. Generate ideas for creating the problem
4. Reverse those ideas to get solutions

## Mind Mapping
- Central concept in middle
- Branches for related ideas
- Sub-branches for details
- Use colors, images, symbols
- Shows connections and hierarchies

## Six Thinking Hats
**White Hat**: Facts, data, information
**Red Hat**: Emotions, feelings, intuition
**Black Hat**: Caution, criticism, risks
**Yellow Hat**: Benefits, optimism, value
**Green Hat**: Creativity, alternatives, growth
**Blue Hat**: Process control, organization, overview

## RAPID (Rapid Ideation)
- Set strict time limits (e.g., 5-10 minutes)
- Generate as many ideas as possible
- No discussion or judgment during ideation
- Review and categorize after time expires
- Good for warming up or breaking blocks
